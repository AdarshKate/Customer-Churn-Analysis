# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn to understand customer behavior, identify patterns associated with customer attrition, and generate actionable business insights to support customer retention.

The project combines data cleaning using Python, business analysis using SQL, and interactive data visualization using Power BI.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer churn patterns and trends.
- Clean and prepare the dataset for analysis.
- Answer business questions using SQL queries.
- Identify customer segments that may be more likely to churn.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard to visualize key metrics.
- Provide data-driven insights to support customer retention strategies.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** – Dataset storage and initial exploration
- **Python (Pandas, NumPy, Jupyter Notebook)** – Data cleaning and preprocessing
- **SQL** – Business analysis and answering business questions
- **Microsoft Power BI** – Interactive dashboard development and visualization

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. **Data Understanding:** Explored the customer churn dataset and its attributes.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Used Python to inspect, clean, and prepare the data for analysis.
3. **SQL Analysis:** Developed SQL queries to investigate customer churn and answer business questions.
4. **Dashboard Development:** Built a Power BI dashboard to visualize key performance indicators and churn patterns.
5. **Business Insights:** Identified potential opportunities to improve customer retention based on the analysis.

## 🧩 Business Problems

This project addresses important business questions to help understand customer attrition and support data-driven retention decisions.

### 1. Customer Churn Overview

- What is the overall customer churn rate?
- How many customers have churned compared with those who have stayed?
- What does the overall customer retention picture look like?

### 2. Customer Segmentation

- Which customer segments experience higher churn?
- How does churn vary across different contract types?
- Are there differences in churn across customer categories, such as senior citizens or customers with dependents, if these fields are available?

### 3. Customer Tenure and Retention

- How does customer tenure relate to churn?
- Are newer customers more likely to leave than long-term customers?
- Which customer groups may need additional retention support?

### 4. Pricing and Payment Behavior

- How is churn associated with monthly charges and total charges?
- Does churn vary by payment method?
- Are particular billing or payment patterns associated with higher churn?

### 5. Services and Customer Behavior

- How does churn vary across internet service types?
- Are customers using particular services more likely to churn?
- Which service combinations or customer characteristics are worth investigating further?

### 6. Business Decision-Making

- Which customer segments should be prioritized for retention initiatives?
- What areas should the business investigate to understand the reasons behind churn?
- How can customer data support more targeted retention strategies?

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis aims to uncover:

- Overall customer churn and retention patterns.
- Customer segments with higher churn rates.
- Relationships between churn, tenure, contract type, and billing behavior.
- Potential areas for customer retention improvement.

## 💼 Business Recommendations

Based on the actual analytical findings, potential recommendations may include:

- Developing targeted retention campaigns for customer segments with high churn.
- Improving onboarding and engagement for newer customers if they show higher churn.
- Reviewing contract and billing options associated with customer attrition.
- Monitoring churn metrics regularly to evaluate retention efforts.
